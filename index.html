2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide you with a smooth, enjoyable gameplay experience, to serve relevant advertisements, to sync your progress safely, and to process legitimate contest or prize claims.

A. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version (Android), unique device identifiers, IP address, connection type, language settings, and general gameplay telemetry (levels completed, moves taken, time spent).
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) used by Google AdMob to serve personalized and non-personalized advertisements, track ad impressions, and prevent fraud.
  • Crash Logs & Performance Data: Anonymous diagnostic information collected automatically via Firebase Analytics and Google Play Services to identify and fix technical glitches and application crashes.

B. Anonymous Game Progress Data (Firebase)

To allow you to continue your puzzle progress seamlessly across sessions and offline states, the app uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication. A randomized, unique User Identifier (UID) is generated. We associate your in-game coins, current level, solved puzzle count, and undo/hint inventory with this UID in the Firebase Realtime Database. This identifier contains no personally identifiable information (such as your real name, email, or telephone number).

C. Grand Prize Claim Information (Voluntary)

Contest & Grand Prize Claim Feature:

Ball Sort Master features a special milestone reward for players who conquer all 1,000,000 puzzle levels. If you achieve this milestone and choose to claim the real-world grand prize, you will be prompted to submit a claim form.

When you voluntarily submit a claim form, we collect the following details:

  • Full Name: To verify winner identity.
  • Email Address: For official communication and verification confirmation.
  • Mobile Phone Number: For delivery coordination and security verification.
  • Full Postal Address & PIN / Zip Code: Solely for physical delivery and shipping of the prize.
  • Cryptographic Verification Hash: A tamper-proof SHA-256 hash confirming that the level milestone was genuinely unlocked within the authentic app binary.

Note: This information is strictly optional and is only collected if you reach the final level milestone and voluntarily choose to submit the claim form. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.
